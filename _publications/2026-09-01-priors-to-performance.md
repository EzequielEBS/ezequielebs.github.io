A major advantage of Bayesian inference is its flexibility in combining information from many sources, including expert opinion and historical data. The literature on Bayesian dynamic borrowing is extensive, but practical implementation guidance is limited. This tutorial reviews common approaches for incorporating external data, including multiple historical datasets, and demonstrates them with publicly available software on tasks such as model selection and average treatment effect estimation.

Examples include logistic regression with the ACTG019/ACTG036 trials and survival analysis with the E1684/E1690 melanoma trials.
