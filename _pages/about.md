I am an M.Sc. student in Applied Mathematics and Data Science at the School of Applied Mathematics of the Getulio Vargas Foundation ([FGV EMAp](https://emap.fgv.br/)) in Rio de Janeiro. My research is in **Bayesian statistics**, especially **power priors** and other methods for borrowing information from historical data, with applications in biostatistics and clinical trials. I also work on **probabilistic forecasting** of infectious diseases.

I work as a research assistant on the [hdbayes](https://journal.r-project.org/articles/RJ-2026-010/) R package project and on the [Mosqlimate](https://mosqlimate.org) project for arbovirus forecasting in Brazil.

I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics at FGV EMAp as a scholarship holder of the Center for the Development of Mathematics and Sciences (CDMC). As an undergraduate, I held research scholarships from PICME and PIBIC, which got me started on graduate-level work, and I served as a teaching assistant five times. I am originally from Arapiraca, Alagoas, where I studied in public schools and won medals three times in the Brazilian Public School Mathematics Olympiad (OBMEP).

Research interests
------
* Bayesian inference and computation
* Power priors and dynamic borrowing of historical data
* Biostatistics and clinical trials
* Epidemiological and sports forecasting
